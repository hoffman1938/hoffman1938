## 👋 About me

I'm a product-minded engineer who works across the whole delivery cycle: gathering requirements, shaping the backlog, building the interface and testing it before it reaches users.

Over 3+ years in fintech I've moved from technical coordination to development to project management, delivering payment software used by Georgian banks. I care about clear requirements, predictable releases and products that work correctly with real money and real customers.

## 🧭 How I work

- **Start from the problem.** Clear requirements and acceptance criteria before any code.
- **Ship in small steps.** Short iterations, visible progress, fewer surprises.
- **Test like a user.** QA habits from day one: edge cases, regressions, real data.
- **Keep everyone aligned.** Business, developers and clients speak through one backlog.
